    Questions

    Before you ask.

    Does ROTATE tell me what to buy?

    No. It shows which themes are speeding up in new launches. The engine’s pick is shown so you can see what it targets, not as advice. Not financial advice.

    Is the board live?

    Yes. On ROTATE’s own site it reads pump.fun’s public launch stream directly in your browser. If the stream can’t be reached, it switches to a simulated preview and every lamp on the page says Preview.

    Why acceleration and not volume?

    Volume and Trending measure what already happened. A theme going from 2% to 9% of launches in ten minutes moves before the charts show it. That gap is the trade.

    How is the leader picked?

    Highest market cap among the theme’s launches in the last hour, from DexScreener’s free public data. If that data can’t be reached, the site says so and shows the latest launch in the theme instead.

    Is the engine buying right now?

    Not yet. The board and the pick run live from launch. The engine wallet goes live after launch, paid by creator fees, and its address is posted here and on X. Until then nothing is bought.

    What can’t it see?

    Names and tickers only. Images, charts and X posts are invisible to it. It counts launches, not quality.

    Will ROTATE DM me?

    Never. No presale, no airdrop, no support account. Anyone messaging you as ROTATE is not us.
